Portfolio Total Return Swap at KKPS

A total return swap gives an institutional client the full economic return of a stock basket, meaning price moves and dividends, without the client buying the shares. KKPS holds the shares as a hedge. The client pays KKPS financing and fees in return.

Total return legKKPS pays the client the basket's price gain and dividends. The client pays KKPS any price loss.
Funding legThe client pays KKPS interest on the notional (reference rate + spread), plus transaction fees.

Where the income comes from

Interest

The funding leg. The client pays a rate on the notional that KKPS finances.

Transaction fees

Charged when the client opens, changes or closes positions in the basket.

Securities lending fees

On short swaps, KKPS borrows the shares to hedge and charges a borrow fee.

One Report p.43 · prices set by the Equity and Derivatives Trading Department

How a swap is valued on the books

Performance flow

Latest bid price of the stock minus the contract's reference price.

Dividend flow

Dividends or capital reductions paid by the underlying stock.

Financing flow

Interest cash flows at the contract rate, discounted on the currency's rate curve.

One Report p.338 · equity linked swap fair value = net of the three flows

Risk controls

Approval before trading

Runs inside a Risk Management Committee framework. The Investment Committee approves before execution.

Hard limits

Limits on investment value and liquidity, VaR and accumulated loss. KKPS Risk Management reports daily. Breaches must be cut back within a set time.

Compliance review

The compliance report covers regulatory compliance for portfolio swap transactions.

Market debate · Oct 2026

Open questions on the HFT flow

Market talk says the TRS client is an HFT firm that ends each day flat. Press reports have not confirmed this or named KKPS. Below is analysis, not a legal opinion.

Regulatory arbitrage?

A margin loan by another name

The funding leg works like a margin loan, but a flat-by-close client barely borrows overnight. The real gain is intraday buying power outside margin-account rules, and orders that reach the market as broker prop, not as an HFT investor. Since 7 Jul 2025, HFT investors may buy only SET100 stocks, DWs, DRs and ETFs.

Who decides?

Client algo, KKPS kill switch

In a true swap the dealer chooses its hedge. If the client's model drives orders in KKPS's prop book, KKPS is still the legal trader and answers for every order. A kill switch alone sits awkwardly with the 56-1's own Investment Committee approval before execution.

How to flag it?

Prop account, foreign flag

SET books TRS hedge trades as foreign (F) inside the prop account. It says the deals break no rules, and is weighing separate P1/P2 accounts with data restated from early 2025. Members meet on 28 Oct. If the flow counts as foreign in the statistics, the open question is whether foreign and HFT rules should apply at order entry too.
